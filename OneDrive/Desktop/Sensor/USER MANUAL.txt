  Live monitoring, alerts and reports for your plant

THIS FOLDER
-----------
  FIRST_TIME_SETUP.bat  Run ONCE on a new PC - one click: installs
                   everything, creates the database and the Super Admin,
                   opens port 5000, starts SentinelEdge and saves the
                   database passwords in "SentinelEdge PASSWORDS - KEEP
                   PRIVATE.txt" (the Super Admin password is never saved).
  START.bat        Starts SentinelEdge and opens the dashboard.
  STOP.bat         Stops SentinelEdge.
  USER MANUAL.txt  This file.
  Manuals\         Installing on a new laptop, Super Admin guide,
                   Excel live reader guide. Manuals\Handover has the
                   Company Admin Manual to give to each company.
  sentineledge\    The program itself. You do not need to open it.


1. STARTING AND STOPPING
------------------------
  * Double-click START.bat. A black window shows the steps; after
    about 10-20 seconds your browser opens the dashboard at
        http://localhost:5000
    Keep the black windows open while you work (you can minimise them).
  * To stop, double-click STOP.bat.
  * If the page does not open, type  http://localhost:5000  in the
    browser yourself.


2. SIGNING IN
-------------
  * Company admin:   http://localhost:5000
      Use the name and password Super Admin created for you.
  * Super Admin:     http://localhost:5000/super-admin/login
      Only for the person who manages companies (see Manuals).
  * Wrong password 5 times locks the sign-in for 15 minutes.


3. WHAT YOU SEE
---------------
  Your company sees ONLY its own data: its own parameters, readings,
  alerts, people (subscribers), logs and reports. Nothing from any
  other company is shown, and yours is never shown to them.

  Dashboard      One tile per parameter your company has (for example
                 only pH, or temperature and pH). Values update live.
  Alerts         Every alert, newest first. Acknowledge an alert here
                 (or from SMS / the mobile app) to stop reminders.
  Analytics      Charts of your parameters over time.
  Live Logs      Your company's activity as it happens: readings,
                 alerts, acknowledgements, sign-ins, settings changes.
  Reports        Daily summary, and EXPORT of all readings between any
                 two dates (CSV or JSON). Nothing is ever deleted
                 automatically, so old dates can always be exported.
  History        Past readings and alerts.
  Subscribers    The people who get alerts (SMS / e-mail / app) and in
                 which order: 1st contact, 2nd contact, ...
  Health         Whether SentinelEdge is working. The Sensor card shows what
                 feeds your dashboard: "Live" (the live collector is sending),
                 "Excel Live" (new rows are arriving in your Excel file),
                 "No Data" (a source is on but nothing new is arriving; the
                 card says why) or "No Live Source" (none switched on in
                 Settings). Nothing is ever shown from old or demo data.
  Settings       See section 4.


4. SETTINGS
-----------
  Parameter limits & alerts
      One row for each parameter your company has. Set the Minimum
      and Maximum. A reading below the minimum or above the maximum
      raises an alert and notifies your subscribers.
      "Alerts" off = readings are still recorded, but no alerts are
      sent for that parameter. Press Save on the row you changed.

  Alert messages (SMS and e-mail)
      Each parameter has its own SMS and its own e-mail. Write what your
      people should read when that parameter goes out of range:
        * SMS   one text box (an SMS is cut at 160 characters).
        * E-mail   a subject and a text.
      Click a placeholder such as {value} to put it in the text; it is
      replaced by the real figure when the alert is sent:
        {name} person   {company}   {parameter}   {unit}   {value} reading
        {limit} limit crossed   {direction} above / below   {severity}
        {time} when (IST)   {min} {max} allowed range
      Example SMS:  {company}: {parameter} is {value}{unit}, {direction}
                    the limit {limit}{unit}. {time}
      Buttons: Preview (shows it with example figures), Use example,
      Save, and Standard message (go back to the built-in wording; an
      empty box always means the standard message). Press "Send test
      alert" above to receive the real message on your own phone.
      Each company's wording is its own: nobody else sees or uses it.

  Tailscale (connecting this computer to the main server)
      Company servers send their data to the main (Super Admin) server, and
      phones reach company servers, over Tailscale (a free private network).
      When you sign in, a notification in the bell (top right) tells you if
      Tailscale is missing, signed out or turned off, or if the main server
      cannot be reached. Settings > Tailscale shows the status and these steps:
        1. Download Tailscale for Windows from tailscale.com/download and
           install it.
        2. Click its icon near the clock > Log in (Google or Microsoft
           account).
        3. Open the invitation link from Super Admin (Settings > Tailscale
           shows it) and press Accept.
        4. In the Tailscale menu turn on "Run unattended" and "Start on
           login", so it stays on after a restart.
        5. Settings > Tailscale > Check again: it turns green.
      An invitation or share link works once per person and expires if not
      used within 30 days; ask Super Admin for a new one.

  Phone app (the same app for every company)
      Everyone installs the same SentinelEdge app. It builds its screens
      from YOUR company: one card per parameter you added, with your own
      limits and units, your live values and your alerts. A company with
      only pH sees only pH; another with temperature and pressure sees
      those. Nothing is built into the app.
        1. Settings > Phone app shows the server address to type
           (this computer's address and port, e.g. 192.168.1.20:5000;
           a Tailscale address works from anywhere).
        2. In the app type that address, then the person's name and PIN
           (set the PIN under Subscribers). A company admin can sign in
           with the admin name and password.
        3. The phone must reach this computer: same Wi-Fi, or Tailscale /
           VPN when away. The address is remembered; to switch to another
           company's server, sign out in the app and enter the new address.
      The app shows the alert, lets the person press Acknowledge, and
      receives alerts as notifications.

  SMS (text messages) - needs a GSM modem
      SMS alerts are sent by a GSM modem plugged into this computer:
      the SIM7600 (SIM7600 EI 4G modem, connected by USB), the module
      SentinelEdge is built and tested with. You need:
        1. the SIM7600 USB modem with its antenna;
        2. a SIM card in it that can send SMS (plan or balance, signal,
           PIN lock off);
        3. the modem's USB driver installed (Device Manager > Ports
           (COM & LPT) shows its COM ports, one of them an "AT" port).
      Plug the modem in, then restart (STOP.bat, START.bat). Settings >
      SMS Configuration shows "Connected" when the modem answers; press
      Send Test (to someone on your Subscribers list) to check. One modem
      serves the whole computer; each company's SMS goes only to its own
      subscribers. SMS is charged by the SIM's plan, 160 characters each.
      Without the modem only e-mail and app alerts are sent.

  E-mail account for alerts
      Alert e-mails and the daily report are sent from YOUR company's
      e-mail account. Nothing is set by default: until you add one, no
      e-mail is sent for your company, and a company never sends from
      another company's mailbox. The main company of the computer has its
      account written into the server's settings file (.env) automatically
      when you press Save (no restart, no editing the file); every other
      company's account is stored encrypted.
      Choose the provider, type the e-mail address and the APP PASSWORD
      (not your normal password), press Save, then "Send test e-mail".
      The password is stored encrypted and is never shown again.
      How to get an app password:
        Gmail:      myaccount.google.com > Security > turn on 2-Step
                    Verification > myaccount.google.com/apppasswords >
                    name it SentinelEdge > Create > copy the 16 letters.
        Outlook.com / Hotmail:  account.microsoft.com > Security >
                    Advanced security options > turn on two-step
                    verification > App passwords > Create.
        Yahoo:      Account Info > Account security > turn on two-step
                    verification > Generate app password.
        Microsoft 365 (work): ask your IT administrator to allow
                    "Authenticated SMTP" for the mailbox (and an app
                    password if two-step verification is on).
        Other:      ask your IT department for the outgoing (SMTP) server,
                    port (587 STARTTLS or 465 SSL) and password.
      If a test fails, the page says why (usually: use the app password,
      not the normal one). SentinelEdge only sends from the account; it
      never reads it.

  Add Parameter / Manage Parameters
      To add a new parameter (for example Flow) press Request
      Parameter. It appears on your dashboard after Super Admin
      approves it. Removing a parameter also needs approval.
      Only APPROVED parameters are ever recorded.

  Excel Live Reader  (how live data comes in)
      Point it at the Excel file your instrument / logger writes, then
      switch the Excel reader ON. Each new row that is added to the
      file is recorded once, checked against your limits and shown
      live. Rows that were already in the file are not imported.
      Full details: Manuals\3 - Excel live reader.txt

  E-mail / SMS configuration
      Send a test e-mail or SMS to check that alerts can reach people
      (only to people already on your Subscribers list).

  Signed in as / Company
      Shows who is signed in and for which company.


5. EVERYDAY TIPS
----------------
  * The dashboard shows "No data" until the first new reading arrives.
  * The clock, server uptime and "x seconds ago" times count live.
  * Alerts for each parameter repeat at most once every 5 minutes
    while the value stays outside the limits.
  * Admin accounts and passwords are created and changed only by
    Super Admin. Ask Super Admin if you forget your password.


6. PROBLEMS
-----------
  Page will not open        Run STOP.bat, then START.bat again.
  Old screens after update  Press Ctrl + F5 in the browser.
  No live readings          Settings > Excel Live Reader: check the
                            file path, press Test File, make sure the
                            Excel reader is ON, and that the column
                            names match your parameter names.
  Not receiving SMS/e-mail  Settings > SMS / E-mail: send a test.
                            Check Subscribers has the right numbers.
